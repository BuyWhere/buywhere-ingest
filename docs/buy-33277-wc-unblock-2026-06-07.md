## Blocker 2 — DB verification queries time out

### Findings
- `products` table: 37.1M rows, 44 GB total, **never ANALYZEd** (`last_analyze = NULL`, `last_autoanalyze = NULL`).
- The `products_created_at_idx` index is `indisvalid = f` and **0 bytes** — a broken/empty index, can't be used by the planner.
