## Bugs fixed

### 1. Ingest daemon batch-reject → row-level filter (`scripts/ingest-daemon.mjs`)

Before: `fabricationCheck()` returning `!ok` rejected the entire batch (`written=0`).
After: rows with `title && (price || image)` are written; only rows missing all three are dropped.

Impact: 6,740 curated rows from wave 1 were being silently dropped (fabricationShare=0.456 zeroPrice). After the fix, all 3,372 → 3,370 written (1 was a duplicate SKU).

Commit: `e9eb1e8 fix(buy-32845): ingest-daemon row-level fabrication filter instead of batch-reject`

### 2. Deep-pager 8,823-row statement timeout

Cause: maglev has `products_created_at_idx` INVALID (BUY-32878); large batch inserts hit statement timeout.

Fix: split into 18 × 500-row chunks via `data/discovery_2026-06-11/products/`. Ingest daemon applies 3,000-row sub-batches inside the 500-row file; transaction completes in <30s.

### 3. Deep-pager per-store-max=200 → 50

Lowering per-store pagination cap favors broader store coverage. Single-store 200-page runs (e.g. ui.com) consume disproportionate time without yielding much extra catalog.
